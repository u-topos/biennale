International Open Call for Artists

 

 

Three Questions

1. What could happen to one of your artworks in your absence?

2. Choose one of your artworks. What must remain unchanged, even if its form, material, and location change?

3. Choose one of your artworks. Write a short instruction that would allow someone else to create a version of it without seeing the original.

The format of your answers is open. Maximum 500 characters per question.

Optional: A link to one existing artwork that may illustrate one of your answers.

 

Conditions of Participation

About the Biennale. U:TOPOS : Biennale 2027 is an independent international contemporary art biennale in Avallon, France. Through dialogue with the artist, the underlying idea of a work is identified and translated into an instruction by the operator. Its execution in the urban environment gives rise to a new version of the work, shaped by the specific location, circumstances, and chance.

Who can participate. Artists from any country with personal experience of migration, exile, displacement, or living between countries and cultures. No documentary proof is required.

Format. Remote collaboration. Instructions are carried out locally in Avallon. Artists are not required to travel, ship artworks, or prepare a new work to apply.

Financial conditions. Applications and participation are entirely free of charge. No artist honoraria or funding for travel or production are provided.

To apply: Your name or artistic pseudonym, country of residence, email address, 1–2 sentences about your artistic practice, and answers to all three questions. No CV, full portfolio, or additional files are required.

Selection. After reviewing the applications, selected artists will be invited to discuss possible participation individually. Applying does not guarantee inclusion in the programme.

Deadline: 1 February 2027.

Selection results. Artists invited for further discussion will be contacted by the end of February 2027.

How to apply: Send your answers to the three questions and the required information about yourself in the body of an email to u.topos.biennale@gmail.com.
